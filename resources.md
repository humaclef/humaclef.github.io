One reason the first edition is a workshop is the abundance of candidate material. Rather than imposing a dataset, the session is a collective space in which the community assesses, compares and prioritises the available resources, so that later shared tasks are community-driven, resource-aware and grounded in real research needs.

## Infrastructures

* [CLARIN](https://www.clarin.eu), including the Virtual Language Observatory.
* [DARIAH](https://www.dariah.eu).
* [Social Sciences and Humanities Open Marketplace](https://marketplace.sshopencloud.eu) at EOSC.
* CLARIAH-ES, the Spanish national node.

* PastReader, at IberLEF, on historical newspapers.
* HISEMOTIONS, at IberLEF, on emotion analysis in historical texts.
