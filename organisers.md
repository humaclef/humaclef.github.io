## Steering committee

A steering committee will provide strategic guidance and support the long-term sustainability of HUMANITAS, bringing together representatives from CLEF, DH initiatives, CH research, and the European infrastructures CLARIN and DARIAH.

The committee is expected to include representatives of the HIPE evaluation community, of the former CHiC evaluation community, and of CLARIN and DARIAH.

## Coordination

The lab chairs oversee all activities: scientific scope, coordination of submissions, and alignment with CLEF procedures and timelines. Data acquisition and preparation build on existing resources and infrastructures, including datasets from initiatives such as PressMint and CLARIAH-ES. Rather than fixing data and evaluation setups in advance, the lab prioritises a community-driven approach in which datasets, annotation practices and evaluation methodologies are jointly assessed during the workshop. For future editions, dedicated task organisers will be appointed as specific evaluation tasks are defined.
